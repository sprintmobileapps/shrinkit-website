For files that won't send, upload or open.

Seven tools, and every one of them works on your phone.

Video compression

Make a big video small enough to send. Choose Max, Recommended or High Quality and see the new size before you start, or pick a target of 10, 25, 50, 100 or 200 MB. Lower the resolution or remove the sound for an even smaller file.

  • MP4 and other phone videos, from HD to 4K
  • Free up to Full HD 1080p
  • Pro keeps 2K and 4K, and makes extra-small files on supported phones

Photo compression

Choose Light, Balanced or Heavy, or set the quality yourself, and save as JPG, PNG or WebP. You see the original and new size side by side, and photos stay upright.

  • JPEG, PNG and WebP
  • Compare before and after with a slider (Pro)
  • Custom quality: a few free tries, unlimited with Pro

Batch compression

Pick photos from your gallery, or choose a folder to add every photo in it, and compress them all in one tap. The batch keeps going if you leave the app.

  • 10 photos at a time on the free plan
  • No limit with Pro, even for folders with thousands of photos
  • Save them all to your gallery or a folder in one tap

PDF compression

Make scanned documents and forms small enough to email or upload. Choose Light, Balanced or Heavy. Password-protected PDFs work too.

  • PDFs up to 10 pages on the free plan
  • Any length with Pro
  • Text in a compressed PDF may not be selectable, and links aren't kept

Unlock PDF

Type a PDF's password once and save a copy that opens in any app without asking again, so bank statements, payslips and e-Aadhaar PDFs open straight away. It also removes print, copy and edit restrictions.

  • Free, with no page limit
  • Remember passwords on this device: 1 free, up to 10 with Pro
  • You need the password. ShrinkIt can't recover a forgotten one.

Form photo and signature resize

Resize a photo or signature to the pixel size and KB limit an online form asks for, from 10 to 200 KB. A camera photo of several MB becomes a small JPG that's as sharp as the limit allows.

  • 11 presets: Passport, Aadhaar, PAN, NEET, UPSC, SSC, RRB, IBPS and university forms
  • Photo and signature sizes
  • Portals change their rules, so check the current size before you submit. ShrinkIt isn't affiliated with any government agency.

Format convert

Convert JPG to PNG, PNG to JPG, WebP to JPG and more, for websites and forms that accept only one format.

  • JPEG, PNG and WebP
  • Set the quality for JPG and WebP
  • Included in the free plan

Your files stay on your phone.

ShrinkIt does all its work on your phone's own processor. There's no server to upload to and no account to create.

  • No uploads

    Your photos, videos and PDFs, and the smaller copies ShrinkIt makes, are never sent anywhere.

  • Only the files you pick

    Files open through Android's own picker, so ShrinkIt sees only what you choose and can't browse your storage.

  • PDF passwords stay private

    A password is saved only if you tick Remember on this device. It's encrypted with a key in Android's Keystore, and never uploaded or backed up.

  • Online only for ads and analytics

    Pro works fully offline. The free version needs a connection so ads can load. The app also sends anonymous usage analytics and checks for settings updates. Read the privacy policy

Questions

Does ShrinkIt upload my files anywhere?

No. ShrinkIt processes everything on your phone, using Android's built-in image, PDF and video tools and its own code. Your files are read from the place you pick, and the compressed or unlocked copy is saved back to your phone. Nothing is sent to a server, and that includes any PDF passwords you type or save.

Does ShrinkIt need an internet connection?

Compressing, resizing, converting and unlocking all run on your phone, so your files never need the internet. Pro works fully offline. The free version needs a connection only so ads can load.

The app also uses the network to send anonymous usage analytics through Firebase, receive occasional notifications, and fetch settings updates from Firebase Remote Config (things like ad frequency and feature limits).

What does Pro include, and is it a subscription?

Pro is a one-time in-app purchase through Google Play, not a subscription. It gives you no ads, 2K and 4K video plus extra-small video files on supported phones, unlimited batch compression (free: 10 photos at a time), PDFs of any length (free: 10 pages), up to 10 saved PDF passwords (free: 1), unlimited custom quality for photos and videos, the before and after slider, keeping photo details, saving to any folder every time, and all future Pro features.

The purchase is permanent and comes back on any device signed in to the same Google account.

Can ShrinkIt remove a password from a PDF?

Yes, if you know the password. Open Unlock PDF, pick the file and type its password once: ShrinkIt saves an unlocked copy that opens without a password in any app. It can also remove print, copy and edit restrictions from PDFs that open without a password. Unlock PDF is free, with no page limit.

ShrinkIt doesn't guess or recover passwords, so if you've forgotten one, ask whoever sent the PDF. The unlocked file is a new copy, so a digital signature on the original (e-Aadhaar has one) doesn't carry over. Keep the original wherever a signed copy is required.

Where are my saved PDF passwords stored?

Only on your phone. A password is saved only if you tick Remember on this device, and only after it has opened the file. It's encrypted with a key held in Android's secure Keystore, kept in ShrinkIt's private storage, left out of device backups, and never uploaded or sent to analytics.

The next time you pick a locked PDF, ShrinkIt tries your saved passwords first, so a monthly statement with the same password opens with nothing to type. Free keeps 1 saved password and Pro keeps up to 10; at the limit, a new one replaces the one you used least recently. You can rename or forget them any time in Settings → Saved PDF passwords, and uninstalling ShrinkIt deletes them.

Does ShrinkIt compress videos?

Yes. ShrinkIt compresses MP4 and other phone videos, including HD and 4K, on your phone. Pick Max, Recommended or High Quality and see the estimated new size before you start, or choose a target size of 10, 25, 50, 100 or 200 MB. You can also lower the resolution or remove the sound for an even smaller file.

The free version saves up to Full HD 1080p; Pro keeps 2K and 4K quality and adds extra-small files on phones that support them. Videos compress one at a time.

Can I compress PDFs without losing text quality?

PDF compression turns each page into a compressed image. Light keeps near-print quality, Balanced is the default, and Heavy gives the smallest file, though very small text may be harder to read. Text in a compressed PDF may not be selectable, and links aren't kept. Scanned and image-heavy PDFs shrink the most; text-only PDFs may not get smaller.

Password-protected PDFs work too: type the password, or let a saved one open the file. Free compresses PDFs up to 10 pages; Pro handles any length.

Which form photo presets are included?

11 presets for Indian online forms: Passport Photo, Aadhaar Update, PAN Card Photo, PAN Card Signature, NEET Photo, UPSC / Govt Form, SSC / Railway, RRB Railway Recruitment, Bank PO (IBPS), University Admission, and a general Signature strip. Pick a preset and your photo, and ShrinkIt resizes it to the right pixel size and KB limit.

Preset sizes follow commonly published portal requirements and can change, so always check the official portal before you submit. ShrinkIt is an independent app and is not affiliated with any government agency.

How do form presets stay under the KB limit?

Each preset has a pixel size and a maximum file size in KB. ShrinkIt resizes your photo to the required size, then finds the highest quality that still fits under the limit, so the photo stays as sharp as the portal allows.

What image formats does ShrinkIt support?

JPEG, PNG (lossless) and WebP. Choose Light, Balanced or Heavy compression, or set the quality yourself. You can also convert between JPG, PNG and WebP with Format Convert. Lossless WebP needs Android 11 or later.

Which languages is ShrinkIt in?

English, Hindi, Bengali, Urdu, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese and Indonesian. ShrinkIt follows your phone's language, and you can choose a different one any time in Settings → Language.

Which Android version do I need?

Android 7.0 or later, on phones and tablets. Files open through the system picker, so ShrinkIt doesn't need broad storage permission.
