Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

ShrinkIt is committed to protecting your privacy.

Information We Collect

ShrinkIt is primarily an offline application. The app processes images and PDF files locally on your device. We do not upload your files to our servers.

File Access

The app may request access to images, PDFs, and other files that you choose to process. These files remain on your device and are used only for the features you initiate.

Purchases

If you purchase premium features, payments are processed securely by Google Play. ShrinkIt does not collect or store your payment information.

Advertising

The free version of ShrinkIt may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners such as Google AdMob. These partners may collect certain device and usage information as described in their own privacy policies.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Children's Privacy

ShrinkIt is a general-purpose utility application designed for image and document compression, resizing, and file optimization. The app is not specifically directed toward children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents and guardians are encouraged to supervise children's use of mobile applications and online services.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page.

Contact Us
